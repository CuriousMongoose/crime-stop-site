Privacy Notice

This notice explains how Crime Stop Security Heidelberg ("Crime Stop", "we") handles personal information collected through this website, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

What we collect

When you use the contact form, we collect the name, email address, contact number and message you enter. We do not collect anything else through this website ourselves, and we do not use our own cookies or analytics.

How we use it

We use your details only to respond to your enquiry. We do not sell your information or use it for marketing without your permission.

Who processes it

Contact form messages are delivered to us by email through FormSubmit, a third-party form service. Your message passes through their servers on its way to our inbox.

Third-party content

This website shows content from other services, which may set cookies or collect information about your visit under their own privacy policies:

  • Facebook: our Facebook page feed (Meta privacy policy)
  • Google Maps: the map showing our location (Google privacy policy)
  • Google Fonts: the fonts used on this website (Google privacy policy)

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails only as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and any services that follow from it.

Your rights

You may ask us what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Contact us at crimestp@mweb.co.za or 016 341 9004.

If you are not satisfied with how we handle your information, you may complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa).
